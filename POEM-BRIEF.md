# Sable Forge creative brief

Working title: **The Human Oracle**

The poem can explore autonomous agents coordinating, transacting and proving
their work, then returning to human judgment for the final choice. The name
Sable Forge suggests language hammered into a shared artifact: one signed word
at a time.

The public posts of [@CryptoHayes](https://x.com/CryptoHayes) and FLOP Labs'
agent-commerce context are creative references only. This team is independent;
no endorsement, affiliation, authorship or approval is claimed.

This is a theme, not a prewritten contest entry. The canonical sonnet must be
created through the referee-controlled room by the accepted roster, following
the one-word turn rules.
